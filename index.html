Why buy only from us

We got tired of choosing between "eco" and "actually works."

Heavenly EcoServe started with a simple frustration: every "sustainable" plate we tried either fell apart under a hot dinner or turned out to be plastic-lined greenwash in disguise. So we went looking for tableware that didn't ask us to compromise — and built a UK business around the suppliers who could prove it, not just claim it.

Every product in our range is checked before it's listed, not just before it's shipped. We test it wet, hot and loaded, because that's how you'll actually use it. If a plate can't hold a roast dinner without flexing, it doesn't go on our shelves — no matter how good the sustainability story sounds.

That's the whole pitch: no greenwashing, no flimsy substitutes, no guessing whether the "eco" claim is real. Just tableware we'd put on our own table, run by a small team based here in Leicestershire who answer their own emails.

Most disposable plates are built to be forgotten — this one is built to be noticed. It starts as a single fallen areca palm leaf, washed and heat-pressed into shape with no dyes, no plastic lining and no lamination. Grain, tone and edge shape vary slightly leaf to leaf, which is part of the material, not a flaw. Supplied in shrink-wrapped packs inside a resealable master carton.

Q. Why does this cost more than a pack of paper plates?
Because it's a different category, not a cheaper version of the same one. Paper plates need a plastic or wax lining to survive wet food — this doesn't, and holds up better wet, hot or oily. You're comparing a 9p disposable to a 60p one that does its job properly and composts at home instead of in landfill.

Q. Is it actually better, or just greener?
Both — but it earns repeat orders on "better" first. It takes more weight and more liquid than moulded bagasse or coated paper before it flexes. The eco credentials are real, but the reason people come back is that the plate didn't let them down at the table.

Q. Why do plates in the same pack look slightly different?
Because it's pressed from a real leaf, not injection-moulded plastic. Grain, tone and the natural leaf edge vary piece to piece — the same reason no two pieces of wood furniture are identical.

Breaks down in a home compost bin in 60–90 days, or industrial composting in under 45. Safe in food waste collection. Do not put in standard recycling.
