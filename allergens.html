Allergens

Allergen information

Please ask us about allergens before you order: our food may contain or be made with nuts and milk products.

If you have a food allergy or intolerance, please tell us when you order and we will confirm the ingredients in every item you choose. Other allergens may be present in our kitchen, so we cannot guarantee that any item is free from any allergen.

Rotis, chapatis and parathas contain wheat (gluten).

Message us on WhatsApp or ask when you order and we will confirm the ingredients and allergens in any item before you buy. We will also confirm them again on delivery.
