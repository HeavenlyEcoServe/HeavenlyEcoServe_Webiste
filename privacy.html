Privacy Policy

How Heavenly EcoServe Ltd collects, uses and protects your personal information. Last updated 10 October 2026.

We are the "controller" of the personal information described in this policy under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. In this policy "we", "us" and "our" mean Heavenly EcoServe Ltd.

2. What information we collect

  • Order and delivery details: your name, phone or WhatsApp number, delivery address and postcode, email address (if you give it), what you order, delivery instructions, allergy information you tell us, and whether and how you have paid.
  • Messages: what you send us on WhatsApp, by email or through the contact form on this website.
  • Orders and payments: when you pay online we keep your order (items, name, email, phone, delivery address and driver instructions, amount and payment status) in a private database and email you and us a confirmation. We keep order records for 6 years for tax and accounting.
  • Enquiries: for catering or tiffin plans: your event date, venue, guest numbers, dietary needs and contact details.
  • Website use on your device: your basket, your chosen colour theme and a "live photo scene" preference are stored on your own device (see section 9). We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.
  • Technical data: like most websites, our hosting provider receives your IP address, browser type and the pages requested so the site can be delivered and kept secure.

We do not knowingly collect special-category data (such as health information) other than allergy or dietary information you choose to give us so that we can serve you safely. We use it only for that purpose.

4. Marketing

We will only send you marketing messages if you have asked us to, or if you are an existing customer and we are promoting similar products (the "soft opt-in" under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003). Every message tells you how to stop. We never sell your details.

5. Who we share it with

  • Delivery: your name, address, phone number and order are shared with whoever delivers your order (our own driver or, in future, a courier partner we will name here).
  • Payments (Stripe): if you pay online, Stripe Payments UK Ltd processes your payment, receives your name, email, phone number, billing details and order amount, and checks payments for fraud under its own privacy policy. We receive confirmation that you have paid, but not your card number.
  • WhatsApp (Meta): if you contact us on WhatsApp, Meta processes your messages under its own privacy policy.
  • Hosting and technology: our website is hosted by Hostinger. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means Google can see your IP address when you load a page.
  • Postcode lookup: when you type a postcode into the basket or contact form, the postcode (not your name or order) is sent to postcodes.io and OpenStreetMap (Nominatim) to check it, suggest postcodes and find the street. If we switch on a full address finder, the postcode is also sent to Ideal Postcodes. These services see your IP address and the postcode. We do not store the lookup.
  • Professional advisers and authorities: accountants, insurers and, where the law requires, HMRC, local authority environmental health officers, trading standards or the police.

These organisations act on our instructions or under their own legal duties. We only share what is needed.

6. International transfers

Some providers above (for example Meta, Google and Cloudflare) may process data outside the UK. Where they do, they rely on safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or adequacy regulations.

7. How long we keep it

  • Order and invoice records: 6 years after the end of the financial year, as tax law requires.
  • Enquiries that do not become orders: up to 12 months.
  • Marketing preferences: until you ask us to stop (we keep a note that you opted out so we do not contact you again).
  • Messages and general correspondence: up to 2 years, then deleted.

8. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • be told how your data is used (this policy)
  • access a copy of your data
  • have wrong data corrected
  • have your data erased (in some cases)
  • restrict or object to our use of it
  • receive your data in a portable format (in some cases)
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier use

To use any right, email heavenlyecoserve@hotmail.com. We will reply within one month and will not charge a fee unless a request is clearly unfounded or excessive. We may need to check your identity first.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or 0303 123 1113.

9. Cookies and similar technologies

This website does not use advertising, tracking or analytics cookies. It uses your browser's local storage to remember:

  • your basket, so it is still there if you reload the page (strictly necessary for the basket to work)
  • your colour theme and 3D-scene setting, if you choose to change them (a preference you ask for)
  • a service worker cache, so the site loads faster and works on a weak signal

You can clear these at any time in your browser settings. If we add analytics or advertising tools in future, we will ask for your consent first and update this page.

10. Keeping your data safe

We use sensible technical and organisational measures: access to customer records is limited to people who need it, our internal systems are password-protected, and the website is served over HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach is likely to put you at risk we will tell you and the ICO as the law requires.

11. Children

Our website and ordering are aimed at adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed. Significant changes will be highlighted on the website.
