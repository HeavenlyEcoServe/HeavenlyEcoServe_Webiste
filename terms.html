3. Allergies and dietary needs

Please ask us about allergens before you order. Our food may contain or be made with nuts and milk products, and other allergens may be present in our kitchen. Tell us about any allergy or intolerance when you order and we will confirm the ingredients in the items you want, and confirm again on delivery. If you are unsure, please do not order.

You can order through the basket on this website (which sends your order to us on WhatsApp), by messaging us on WhatsApp, by phone or through the contact form. Placing an order is an offer to buy. A contract is formed only when we confirm your order (for example by a WhatsApp reply) and the items, price and delivery time are agreed. We may decline an order, for example if an item is unavailable or we cannot deliver to your address, and we will tell you promptly.

5. Prices and payment

Prices are in pounds sterling and include VAT where VAT applies. Prices on the website may change, but a change will not affect an order we have already confirmed. We will tell you the total, including any delivery charge, before you confirm. You can pay online by card (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), Apple Pay, Google Pay or Link through Stripe, or arrange payment with us on WhatsApp. Online payments are taken securely by Stripe Payments UK Ltd: we never see or store your full card details. Your order is confirmed when your payment succeeds and we send you a confirmation email. If we have made an obvious pricing mistake we may correct it before confirming.

  • Delivery times are estimates, not guarantees. We will do our best to be on time and will let you know if we are delayed.
  • Please give a complete and accurate address and phone number, and be available to receive your order. If we cannot reach you or leave the order safely, we may have to cancel it and the cost of the food may still be payable because fresh food cannot be resold.
  • Risk in the food passes to you when it is delivered to you or the person you nominate.

Our food is made fresh to order and is perishable, so under the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013 the 14-day right to cancel does not apply to it. Even so, please contact us as soon as you can if you need to change or cancel an order. If we have not started preparing it, we will normally agree to a change or cancellation. For daily tiffin plans, we will explain any pause or cancellation arrangements when you sign up.

If your food is not as described, is not of satisfactory quality or arrives damaged, please contact us on the day of delivery, with a photo if you can, so we can investigate and put things right.
